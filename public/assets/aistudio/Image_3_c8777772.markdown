## Brand & Style

The design system projects monumental elegance, surgical precision, and bespoke materiality. Designed for high-net-worth clients, developers, and visionary institutions, the interface must feel like an ultra-exclusive architectural monograph combined with a modern atelier workspace.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Movement:** Dark Luxury Editorial with Architectural Tonalism.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating canvas cadences—transitioning deliberately between abyssal deep charcoals and gallery-grade warm off-white limestone surfaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Controlled, high-contrast, serene, and tactile. Uncluttered visual planes anchored by hair-thin 1px structural framing, bronze reflections, and generous spatial discipline.
- **Emotional Response:** Reassurance of mastery, enduring permanence, discreet wealth, and museum-grade discernment.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around deep spatial voids, warm natural aggregate, and muted metallic brass/bronze alloy tones.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Surface Voids (Dark Theme Canvas):** `#0E1116` (Deep Obsidian) to `#12151B` (Rich Basalt). Used for immersive project showcases, cinematic hero states, and architectural floorplans.
- **Alternate Canvas (Gallery Cream):** `#F4EFE8` (Limestone Alabaster). Applied across alternating editorial portfolio sections to evoke raw stone, heavy museum stock, and natural linen.
- **Accents:**
  - Base Bronze: `#B8925A` (Muted Architectural Gold) for critical focal points, active tab lines, key statistics, and micro-rules.
  - Radiant Bronze (Hover): `#D2AE78` for interactive micro-states, button illumination, and focal borders.
- **Framing & Borders:**
  - Dark Surface Borders: `rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.08)` to `rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.25)`.
  - Light Surface Borders: `rgba(14, 17, 22, 0.08)` to `rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.35)`.
- **Text Systems:**
  - Dark Mode: Primary `#F4EFE8`, Secondary `rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.65)`, Muted `rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.40)`.
  - Light Mode: Primary `#0E1116`, Secondary `rgba(14, 17, 22, 0.70)`, Muted `rgba(14, 17, 22, 0.45)`.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of high-contrast transitional serifs with the structural clarity of a wide-tracked geometric sans-serif.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero narrative statements, project monograph titles, and section indices. Rendered predominantly in Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights to preserve the delicate razor-edge serifs.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Inter`):** Set at Light (`300`) and Book (`400`) weights with deliberate leading (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) to ensure readability against dark obsidian and cream stone backgrounds.
- **Architectural UI Labels & Badges (`Inter` Caps):** Every badge, metadata tag, navigational anchor, and status label must be set strictly in uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.12em` to `0.20em`), echoing blueprint title blocks and exhibition plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column architectural grid with structural axis lines and generous negative space reminiscent of fine art folio publications.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin-lg` (5rem / 80px) outer framing, `gutter-lg` (2.5rem / 40px). Max page containment at `1440px` with optional full-bleed panoramic media strips.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin-md` (3rem / 48px) margins, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1rem / 16px).

### Section Alternation Matrix
Sections alternate rhythmically between full-height Dark Basalt voids (`#0E1116` / `#12151B`) and Full Light Limestone panels (`#F4EFE8`). Transitions between themes occur edge-to-edge with no soft gradients—relying on razor-sharp horizontal divider rules (`1px` tinted line) or direct canvas shifts to emulate entering distinct architectural pavilions.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth avoids soft, generic blurs. It relies on subtle surface layering, micro-thin hairline boundaries, and restrained directional bronze halos.

### Visual Depth Hierarchy
1. **Foundation Level (Flat Voids):** Backgrounds at `#0E1116` or `#F4EFE8`. No elevation.
2. **Structural Tonal Layers:**
   - Dark Tier 1: `#12151B`
   - Dark Tier 2 (Elevated Planes & Drawer Shells): `#171B23`
   - Cream Elevated Plates: `#FFFFFF`
3. **Ghost Boundaries & Hairlines:** Crisp `1px` borders are mandatory across cards, headers, and division walls. Dark mode uses `border: 1px solid rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.08)`. Hover states selectively brighten to `rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.40)`.
4. **Architectural Diffused Shadows:**
   - Ambient Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` with an embedded subtle gold warm tone.
   - Project Specimen Shadow: `0 30px 60px -20px rgba(7, 9, 12, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined radius scale that softens geometric rigidity without sacrificing architectural precision.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Radius (`roundedness: 2` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for micro-inputs, buttons, tag chips, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`):** Used for major structural cards, project galleries, and floating navigation panels.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Applied to modal sheets and full-bleed image viewports.
- **Architectural Exceptions:** Floorplan viewports and data matrices maintain crisp `0px` corners to reinforce drafted precision when embedded within parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Bronze Action:**
  - Background: `#B8925A`
  - Text: `#0E1116`, `label-caps` font style, bold uppercase.
  - Hover: Background fills to `#D2AE78` with a subtle elevation shift (`0 4px 20px rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.25)`).
  - Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`, radius `8px`.
- **Secondary Ghost Outline:**
  - Background: Transparent.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.4)`.
  - Text: `#F4EFE8` (in dark canvas) or `#0E1116` (in light canvas).
  - Hover: Border switches to `#D2AE78` with a `rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.06)` backdrop wash.

### Cards & Monograph Tiles
- Built with a structural `1px` border (`rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.08)` on dark, `rgba(14, 17, 22, 0.08)` on light).
- Radius is fixed at `14px` to `16px`.
- Internal image containers showcase a slow zoom-in on hover (`scale(1.03)` with cubic-bezier `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing).
- Footers feature bronze metadata indices (e.g., `EST. 2024 / RESIDENTIAL / KYOTO`) in `label-caps`.

### Input Fields & Selection Controls
- **Inputs:** Dark field background `#12151B` encased in a `1px` border (`rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.12)`). Active focus shifts the border to `#B8925A` with zero outer ring glow. Caret color is fixed to `#D2AE78`. Text typed is crisp `#F4EFE8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp custom glyphs with `1px` bronze strokes. Checked state fills with `#B8925A` bearing a deep charcoal glyph mark.

### Architectural Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`0.35rem 0.85rem`), radius `4px` to `6px`.
- Caps lock lettering (`label-caps`), wide letter tracking (`0.18em`).
- Dark mode: `background: rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.08)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(184, 146, 90, 0.25)`, `color: #D2AE78`.

### Blueprint & Plan Data Lists
- Horizontal rows separated by hair-thin `1px` lines (`rgba(244, 239, 232, 0.06)`).
- Key-value pairs display architectural parameters (e.g., "GROSS INTERNAL AREA", "STRUCTURAL TIMBER", "COMPLETION"). Values are highlighted in `#F4EFE8` or `#B8925A`.
